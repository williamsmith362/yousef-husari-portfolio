im yousef husari, an 18 year old self taught bespoke designer.

since i was 16, i have been teaching myself how to produce my own clothing from fabric to finish.

i design, cut, and sew pants, jackets, hoodies, bags, pouches, totes and everyday items. sky’s the limit.

i work with an industrial juki 5550n-7, juki w654 mirai, singer hd, an industrial iron, and a japanese grommet press for most of my projects.

i primarily work with heavy fabrics, around 13-15 oz denim/canvas.

thank you.
