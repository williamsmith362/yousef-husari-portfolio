    Measuring references Where the methods come from

    Original illustrations and instructions adapted to this calculator’s fields. Our shared vertical reference is the top of the intended waistband. A natural-waist draft uses the natural waist; a lower-rise draft needs its own marked starting level.

    • FreeSewing — waist measurement
    • FreeSewing — full seat
    • FreeSewing — seated crotch depth
    • NMSU — pants measurements and ease
    • Todd Shelton — measuring finished jeans
    • Williamsburg Garment Company — jeans measurements
    • Luxire — outside length
    • FreeSewing — waist-to-seat height

    Garment brands use different measuring stations. Record your own seat and knee positions so future measurements stay comparable. The illustrations are a guide to tape placement, not a pattern or a fit guarantee.
